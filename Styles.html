Design Styles & Aesthetics

Exploring various design philosophy trends allows you to discover the specific aesthetic that resonates with your personal lifestyle and home environment.

Harmonious & Warm

Japandi Style

A fusion of Scandinavian functionality and Japanese rustic minimalism. It focuses on clean lines, natural materials like light woods, and uncluttered spaces with warm neutral tones.

Essential & Clean

Minimalism

Centered around the philosophy of "Less is More." It emphasizes simple forms, hidden storage, neutral palettes, and eliminating unnecessary clutter to highlight spatial freedom.

Raw & Urban

Industrial Style

Inspired by converted warehouses and urban lofts. Features exposed brick walls, metal accents, raw wood elements, and a neutral-to-dark color scheme with a bold character.

Cozy & Timeless

Modern Farmhouse

Combines rustic country warmth with modern crisp aesthetics. Characterized by neutral backdrops, comfortable slipcovered furniture, textured weaves, and antique touches.

Pro Tip: You don't have to strictly stick to one style. Mixing 70% of a primary style with 30% of another creates a unique, personalized room with depth.
