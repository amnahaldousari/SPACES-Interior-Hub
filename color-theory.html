The Psychology of Color

Colors set the emotional tone of any room. Understanding how shades interact allows you to balance warmth, light, and depth without overspending on decor.

The 60-30-10 Rule

A classic interior design proportion rule that creates balance and harmony in any space:

60%

Dominant Color
Walls, large rugs, or main sofas.

30%

Secondary Color
Curtains, accent chairs, or bedding.

10%

Accent Color
Cushions, artwork, or decorative vases.
